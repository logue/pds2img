# Playground

Choose **PDS3** or **PDS4**, then select the label file and the image file. The converted image appears below the form, and you can download it as a 16-bit PNG or TIFF. Everything runs locally in your browser.

No data at hand? Use the sample links in each tab to download a product from the PDS Imaging Node, or see [Getting started](/pds2img/guide/start/getting-started.md#get-some-data).
